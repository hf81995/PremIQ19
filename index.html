How To Play - Slots

All PL teams will have played each other once by early January. See fixtures here.

Your job is to decide the slot (finishing position) for 7 teams, at the point where all teams have played each other once.*

A team to finish in slot:

  • 1st
  • 2nd
  • Within 3rd to 6th
  • Within 7th to 12th
  • Within 13th to 18th
  • 19th
  • 20th

Scoring:

Team win = 3 points. Goal scored = 2 points.

Multiplier:

Each slot has a different multiplier attached to it, from x1.5 up to x5, meaning if your selected team finishes in the correct slot, points from wins and goals are multiplied.

If a selected team finishes outside the correct slot, all points from wins and goals scored are wiped, and your selected team will receive a flat 50 points with no multiplier.

*In 25/26 all teams had played each other once by Matchweek 20, early January. The reason that it wasn't 19 opponents in 19 games was due to some rescheduling in Matchweek 15 (due to European schedules, etc).

PREMiQ19's results and table will be decided as per points from each team playing opponents for the first time only, and should end around early Jan. The PREMiQ19 table will mirror the official PL table, until the possible eventuality that a rescheduling takes place, and at that point the table will only provide goals and results from first-time fixtures only.
