How To Play - Slots

All PL teams will have played each other once by early January. See fixtures here.

Your job is to decide the slot (finishing position) for 7 teams, at the point where all teams have played each other once.*

A team to finish in slot:

  • 1st
  • 2nd
  • Within 3rd to 6th
  • Within 7th to 12th
  • Within 13th to 18th
  • 19th
  • 20th

Scoring:

Team win = 3 points. Goal scored = 2 points.

Multiplier:

Each slot has a different multiplier attached to it, from x1.5 up to x5, meaning if your selected team finishes in the correct slot, points from wins and goals are multiplied.

If a selected team finishes outside the correct slot, all points from wins and goals scored are wiped, and your selected team will receive a flat 50 points with no multiplier.

*In 25/26 all teams had played each other once by Matchweek 20, early January. The reason that it wasn't 19 opponents in 19 games was due to some rescheduling in Matchweek 15 (due to European schedules, etc).

PREMiQ19's results and table will be decided as per points from each team playing opponents for the first time only, and should end around early Jan. The PREMiQ19 table will mirror the official PL table, until the possible eventuality that a rescheduling takes place, and at that point the table will only provide goals and results from first-time fixtures only.

💡 Reminder: Match win = 3 points, Goal scored = 2 points

How To Play - Blackjack

Whichever team you selected in Slot 1st Place, you now have a big call to make at the Blackjack table.

Select a minimum of 3 players (max. of 5) from that side to score 21 goals between them in 19 fixtures. Use the squad list on the right hand side — which also tells you their total goals from the 25/26 season — and type names onto the playing cards.

How To Play - Monthly Super Sunday Roulette

Every month, one fixture is chosen for all players. Whatever your points from Slots are at that point, you head to the roulette table and spend points on 7 different bets — a minimum of 100 points, up to a maximum of 150.

5 chips are mandatory — you must use these:

25pt chip — ResultEvens, win 50
10pt chip — Scoreline10/1, win 100
25pt chip — Both Teams To ScoreEvens, win 50
20pt chip — Most CornersEvens, win 40
20pt chip — Most Shots on TargetEvens, win 40

2 chips are optional — higher risk, your choice:

25pt chip — Red Card in the Match4/1, win 100
25pt chip — Penalty in the MatchEvens, win 50

Drag each chip onto its matching spot on the table to place your bet, just like a real roulette table — then make your prediction for that category.

This is a single-game prediction, resolved once that month's fixture is played.
